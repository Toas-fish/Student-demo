# Chinese Culinary History: From AI to Historical Evidence
# 中国饮食史：从 AI 到史料

This website is an interactive educational tool designed to demonstrate a critical learning methodology: **how to cross-verify AI-generated content with real historical evidence.**

本网页是一个互动式教学工具，旨在展示一种批判性的学习方法：**如何使用真实的历史史料来交叉验证 AI 生成的内容。**

## 📖 What does this website do? / 网页功能简介

1. **AI Hallucination Showcase (Interactive Cards)**
   The carousel on the homepage presents 5 common historical misconceptions generated by AI. Clicking on a card reveals a side-by-side comparison: the flawed AI statement on the left, and the real historical evidence (with citations) on the right.
   *首页的轮播卡片展示了5个 AI 常见的“历史幻觉”错误。点击卡片可以进入详情页，左右对比查看“AI 的瑕疵回答”与“真实史料考证”。*

2. **Search & Verify (Interactive Search)**
   You can search for specific historical topics. The system will output a pre-generated AI response based on keywords, while simultaneously providing a Google Search and Gemini button. This encourages users to find real academic support and brings critical verification into their future AI usage.
   *在搜索页，你可以输入特定主题。系统会根据关键词输出预设的 AI 回答，同时提供 Google 搜索和 Gemini 跳转按钮。这不仅鼓励用户去寻找真实的学术支撑，更希望培养他们将这种求证思维带入未来的 AI 使用中。*
   
4. **Bilingual Support**
   The entire website supports switching between Chinese and English.
   *全站支持中英文切换*

## 🚀 How to use it? / 使用指南

- **Step 1**: Hover over the auto-scrolling cards on the homepage to pause, and click on any card to see the "AI vs. History" comparison.
  *(将鼠标悬停在首页自动滚动的卡片上即可暂停，点击任意卡片查看“AI vs. 史料”对比。)*
- **Step 2**: Click the "Search Dishes" button to enter the search page.
  *(点击首页的搜索按钮进入搜索页。)*
- **Step 3**: Enter keywords and press Enter. Click the Google button to verify the facts.
  *(输入关键词并回车，点击 Google 按钮去核实事实。)*
